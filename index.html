Explore Cumnor Hurst

A place to walk, discover and look after.

Cumnor Hurst is a special landscape of woodland, grassland and paths, enjoyed by local people and visitors throughout the year. It is owned by the Parish Council and cared for with the help of local volunteers.

Use the interactive map to find your way around, then explore the stories, wildlife and conservation work that make the Hurst special.

Looking after the Hurst

Conservation is a community effort.

As an SSSI, Cumnor Hurst is recognised for its special wildlife and habitats. Looking after those habitats takes regular work: managing vegetation, keeping paths usable, monitoring wildlife and responding to changing conditions.
